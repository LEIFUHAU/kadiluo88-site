FAQ

What is the MOQ for this bottle?

For stock molds, MOQ is 2,000 pcs. If you need a custom shape or dedicated mold, MOQ is 30,000–50,000 pcs depending on the design.

How long is the sampling time?

Stock bottle samples ship in 3–5 days. If you need a custom finish applied, allow 7–15 days.

Do you offer custom pump, cap and sprayer?

Yes. We supply 15mm crimp pumps (aluminium / black / gold), over-caps, acrylic and wood caps as matching accessories.

Can you do a custom bottle shape for my brand?

Yes. Send a 3D drawing or physical sample. Tooling cost is quoted per drawing, and the lead time is 30–45 days for a new mold.
